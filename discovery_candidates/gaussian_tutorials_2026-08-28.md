# Discovered tutorial/workshop sources for gaussian -- 2026-08-28

Deep pass on university teaching material (lecture slides/notes, lab handouts, GaussView
"getting started" guides, workshop material) for the gaussian domain, worldwide, distinct
from HPC-center software documentation. Cast a wide net across the US, Canada, UK,
France, Germany, Spain, Colombia, India, Japan, and beyond. All GOOD candidates below
were verified by reading the actual extracted text, not just trusting the keyword count --
checked specifically for the "wrong Gaussian" trap (statistics/ML/optics use of the word)
and for thin stub pages.
